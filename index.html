Welcome to Our Neighborhood Bakery

Good morning from North Star Bakery. We bake bread, pastries, and cakes for local families, commuters, and anyone who wants something homemade.

What We Are Known For

We are known for fresh sourdough, flaky morning pastries, and custom cakes for birthdays and events. Everything is mixed and baked in our neighborhood kitchen.
