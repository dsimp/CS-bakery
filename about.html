Our Story

North Star Bakery started as a small neighborhood shop. We wanted a place where people could pick up bread before work and order cakes for family events without going across town.

How We Source Ingredients

We buy flour from regional mills when we can and use local eggs and dairy. We keep the ingredient list short so the bread still tastes like bread.

Staff Highlights

Maya runs the early bread shift. Jordan handles pastry and cake orders. Sam works the counter and helps with weekend pickup times.
